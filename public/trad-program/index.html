He who finds a wife finds a good thing, and obtains favor from the Lord. Proverbs 18:22

RichardShulamite

1Ceremony introduction

Richard's family arrives and is formally welcomed by Shulamite's family. Members of both families are introduced.

2Purpose of visit stated

An elder from Richard's family formally explains why they have come: to ask for Shulamite's hand in marriage.

3Presentation of the dowry

The groomsmen bring in the carefully prepared dowry items and present them to Shulamite's family for inspection.

4Entrance of the groom

Richard makes his joyful entrance, accompanied by his groomsmen, as both families welcome him in celebration.

5Bride's grand entrance

Following the family dialogue, Shulamite makes her grand entrance surrounded by her bridesmaids.

6Identification of the bride

An elder asks Richard to confirm that Shulamite is the woman he intends to marry. Richard proudly confirms his choice.

7Consent and acceptance of dowry

Shulamite's father asks her three times whether the family should accept the gifts. With her consent, the family formally accepts the dowry.

8Blessing, vows and rings

The pastor blesses the marriage, leads Richard and Shulamite in their vows, and officiates the exchange of rings.

9Declaration of marriage

The marriage is formally declared complete, and Richard and Shulamite are celebrated as husband and wife.

10Words of wisdom and advice

Representatives from both families offer heartfelt counsel, encouragement and blessings to the couple as they begin their marriage.
